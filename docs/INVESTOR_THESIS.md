# Scholarz Nation — investor thesis (claims separated from hypotheses)

## Thesis

Scholarz may be able to become the trusted academic opportunity intelligence and progress layer for African learners. The differentiator is not AI-written scholarship news; it is source provenance, local eligibility context, student-contributed reality, structured records and distribution in existing student workflows.

## What we can legitimately claim now

- ApplyMate has an existing Blogger interface and an uploaded theme with complex interaction patterns.
- A separate Cloudflare Scholarz preview was previously launched; the rebuild here is a distinct, locally tested architecture prototype.
- A Google AI Courses sample article has structured source/claim data and official application links.
- The editorial workflow and release gates are specified; a local mock harness shows the desired flow but does not yet autonomously conduct research or publish.

## Hypotheses to test (not verified market facts)

- Students return because local context and eligibility reduce uncertainty.
- Institutions/partners may pay for measurable reach or verified application services, while basic access remains free.
- Learners may adopt country reports and academic tools more readily than paid static notes.
- A narrow, human-approved AI editorial engine can maintain quality at lower marginal cost than a fully manual newsroom.

## Defensibility (must be earned)

1. **Provenance graph:** claims linked to official sources and update history.
2. **Local outcome knowledge:** consent-based contributor data, verified costs and barriers, useful at country/institution/program level.
3. **Distribution:** direct channel subscriptions plus valuable public search traffic, not purchased vanity audience.
4. **Compounding workflows:** opportunity actions, reminders, corrections and study preparation connect over time.
5. **Trust:** measurable source accuracy, correction speed and no misleading credentials.

All five are execution moats, not proprietary technology moats. They weaken if Scholarz becomes an unsupervised AI news farm.

## Early scoreboard

- **Demand:** returning readers, search-to-official-click rate, weekly active users, opportunity saves, newsletter/WhatsApp opt-in quality.
- **Trust:** sourced-claim coverage, stale-open rate, correction turnaround, false deadline/fee incidents.
- **Outcomes:** verified user-reported application submissions, scholarship interviews/acceptances only with consent and evidence; avoid claims of causing selection.
- **Unit economics:** cost per verified opportunity, cost per useful user action, human review minutes/post, AI retries, source uptime.
- **Commercial:** experimental sponsor demand and conversion, excluding deceptive ranking boosts.

## Investor release requirements

No investor-facing traction claims without analytics audit trail. No unit-economics presentation until costs are recorded over a meaningful cohort. No "millions of potential students" substituted for evidence of actual engagement. Ask for a specific milestone budget and show what it will prove.
