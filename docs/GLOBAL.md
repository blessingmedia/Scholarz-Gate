# Scholarz Global — country and student-intelligence foundation

`global.scholarz` is a proposed future hostname, not an active service. Build data governance before setting DNS.

## The user promise

"Learn what studying abroad actually looks like before you spend money or board a plane." Country guides should cover admissions channels, scholarship conditions, visas, recognized providers, budgeting evidence, housing constraints, arrival steps and actual experiences — with region/date context and clear uncertainty.

## Entity model

Country -> city -> institution -> program -> opportunity -> requirement; contributor reports link to time, currency, evidence and consent. Keep admission/visa/financial requirements as source-checked factual claims, **not** anonymous community assertions.

## No fake expertise

Every "Scholarz in [country]" report needs direct contributor consent, verification that the contributor actually has the claimed relationship (privacy-preserving), independent fact checking, removal rights and a correction route. Do not fabricate ambassadors, enrollment successes, approvals or visa statistics.

## Evidence confidence

- Official government/university facts: check source and update regularly.
- Student experience: present as anecdotal, location/time-scoped.
- Cost of living: show date, city, basket, currency and sample size where available; otherwise say unavailable.
- School recognition/accreditation: link issuing body, never improvise.

## Validation milestones before spinout

1. At least 2 validated country guides with complete citations and actionable checklists.
2. At least 5 consent-based contributor reports with evidence and no identifying details released without consent.
3. Documented correction and challenge process.
4. Real students demonstrate the guide changes a decision, reduces research time or prevents a poor application.

Numbers above are internal proposed release criteria, not claims about current activity.
